## 4. Results — how should they be released?

- The system **keeps results sealed** (nobody, even admins, sees vote counts
  until you release them). Recommended: the committee announces/publishes after
  polls close. Confirm **who is allowed to publish results** (normally the
  Electoral Committee + President) and that this is OK.
- Say if you instead want results to appear automatically the moment voting
  closes (less recommended — no time for checks).
